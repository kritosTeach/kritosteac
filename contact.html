Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in any course by clicking the "Enroll Now" button on the course page. You'll be redirected to our secure payment portal.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all our courses. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team for a full refund.

Are the courses self-paced?

Yes, all our courses are self-paced. You can learn at your own speed and access the materials 24/7 from any device.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes, upon completion of any course, you'll receive a certificate of completion that you can add to your LinkedIn profile or resume.

How long do I have access to the course?

You have lifetime access to all course materials, including future updates and additions.

Can I access courses on mobile devices?

Absolutely! Our platform is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
